Questions we hear

Small questions, clear answers.

Beside every question, inside every moment. ♡
What is Storkino?

A mobile app that brings together tracking baby care, coordinating with family, and preserving special moments in one experience.

Will it be on platforms other than iPhone?

Storkino is being built for iPhone first. No timeline for Android yet; join the early access list to stay in the loop.

Can family members use it together?

Yes. You can share handoff notes and care info with family and caregivers. End-to-end encrypted family sync will be optional in the full release.

Is my data safe?

Records live on your phone. If you turn on family sync, records are encrypted before they leave; our server only holds encrypted data. No ads, no tracking. Details in our privacy policy.
